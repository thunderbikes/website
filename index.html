UBC ThunderBikes

Interested in motorcycles, racing, or electric vehicles? Join UBC’s electric motorcycle engineering team!

UBC ThunderBikes designs, builds, and tests high-performance electric racing motorcycles, aiming to compete across North America.

Hands-on Innovation:

Design, build, test, and refine real solutions on an electric motorcycle. Gain technical skills comparable to senior co-op terms with the freedom to experiment.

Learn Essential Skills:

Manage deadlines, budgets, teamwork, and communication—all while balancing university life and co-op. Develop problem-solving abilities applicable far beyond the bike.

Ride the Future:

Be part of building a high-performance machine that showcases your work at competitions.
